# Garden illustration

`enchanted-garden.png` is the production artwork used by `index.html` and `garden.css`.
Generated with the built-in `image_gen` tool; 1536 × 1024 pixels. The generated image is copied into this project so deployment does not depend on a local Codex path.

## Final generation prompt

Use case: illustration-story. Asset type: production background artwork for a Vietnamese second-grade classroom emotional wellbeing website. Create a stunning premium 3D storybook illustration, landscape 3:2 composition. A single majestic whimsical round-canopy tree centered at x50%, trunk base at y87%, crown spans x15%-85% and y8%-63%. The tree has rich layered tiny soft moss-like leaves, lush emerald and spring green rounded foliage clusters, delicate white and blush pink blossoms, beautifully sculpted warm twisting brown trunk with branching arms. A magical sunlit meadow of lush grass, tiny daisies and pastel flowers in foreground, rolling green hills behind, soft turquoise blue sky and fluffy dimensional white clouds. Very subtle rainbow arc in far upper left, a few tiny butterflies. Charming polished animated feature film quality, tactile miniature diorama, ambient occlusion, cinematic golden morning rim light, airy luminous atmosphere, depth of field on distant landscape, enchanting and reassuring for 7 year old children. Tree fully visible with space around it; tree crown softly detailed enough to overlay small emotion icons. Professional composition, exquisite surface detail, balanced soft vibrant colors, not flat vector art. NO text, NO letters, NO emoji faces, NO UI, NO watermarks, NO people.

## Animated classroom characters

`garden-friends.png` is a transparent 2 × 2 atlas (deer, hedgehog, squirrel, letter A), rendered with the built-in `image_gen` tool from the teacher-provided `connai.webp`, `connhim.webp`, and `chuA.webp`. CSS selects quadrants without changing the original references.

Final generation prompt: Create one square transparent 2 × 2 character atlas, with equal invisible quadrants and transparent gutters: top left the orange spotted baby deer from connai.webp; top right the hedgehog carrying apples and a pear from connhim.webp; bottom left an orange squirrel inspired by connai.webp; bottom right the friendly red uppercase A with cap, white gloves and sneakers from chuA.webp. Preserve identities and colors in a polished children's storybook style. Each character fully visible, centered in its quadrant with generous margin. No forest, ground, rainbow, frames, grid, words or watermark. Actual transparent background.
